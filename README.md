# ZNEPro benchmarks

Public hardware studies from the ZNEPro team. Each study is self-contained and can be rebuilt offline.

## Studies

- [Rigetti Cepheus-1-108Q, engine 0.1.3](rigetti/cepheus-1-108q/engine-0.1.3/README.md). Three rounds on Cepheus, scored without a calibration card. The main denominator is 25 pre-registered cases: 20 of 25 are better than raw in all three rounds, and 21 of 25 have a lower three-round mean absolute error.
